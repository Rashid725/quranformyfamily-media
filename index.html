QuranForMyFamily Publisher

A personal tool that publishes Quran recitation videos (complete surahs and passages of complete verses, recited by Sheikh Maher Al-Muaiqly, with the Madinah Mushaf text of the King Fahd Complex) to its owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts, QuranForMyFamily, using each platform's official API. It runs on the owner's computer and is used by no one else.
